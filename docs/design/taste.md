# Taste

The judgment tier: character and expression, beyond the rules the other references state as
must/must-not. These are not pass/fail checks — they are how a correct page becomes a good one. Read
[Foundations](foundations.md) first.

## Be expressive with feature data

A headline figure or a distribution is a chance to make something, not just a number in a card. When a
data point is the reason the user opened the page, give it room and confidence: a fleet-health
breakdown can be a full-width band with its segments in their status colors, a divider above and space
below, so it becomes a feature of the page rather than a widget stuffed in a card. Spend the space
where the data earns it.

## Put craft inside the card, not just an edge around it

Reaching for a card is the easy half; what goes in it is the hard half. A card that frames a pile of
unrelated inputs is worse than no card — the frame promises a structure the contents don't deliver.
Give the inside a clear title, a sensible order, dividers that group, and a hierarchy that steps from
most important to least. An edge is worth nothing without order within it.

## Use a bold title

A section title earns its keep by letting the reader pick the section out at a glance. Make it bold —
heavier, not necessarily bigger. Weight is cheaper than size and signals intent; it turns a run of
unrelated inputs into named, scannable parts.

## Compose the space deliberately

Don't strand sparse data across a wide row, and don't default to one stacked column of full-width
cards — that is the first answer, rarely the best one. Look at what the content is, then choose its
shape: a two-column split, a grid of compact cards, a narrow rail of links beside the main column. The
layout is a decision, not a default.

## The reference family

The aesthetic north star is the quiet developer-tool family — Linear, Vercel, shadcn, Midday, Clerk,
Raycast: calm, measured, restrained with color, confident with type. When a page is correct but not
yet good and no rule explains why, hold it against that family and ask what they would cut.
